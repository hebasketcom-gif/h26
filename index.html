About Dr. Samala Sri Kavya

Bridging Clinical Practice and Pharmaceutical Safety

Dr. Samala Sri Kavya is a dedicated Medical Reviewer whose professional path spans clinical dentistry, medical document evaluation, drug safety monitoring, pharmacovigilance, and clinical research.

Having cultivated a foundational understanding of patient diagnostics, physiology, and therapeutic care through dentistry, Dr. Samala Sri Kavya transitioned into the specialized domain of medical review. This evolution connects hands-on clinical knowledge with analytical safety evaluation and evidence-based pharmaceutical review.

Her focus centers on evaluating medical data integrity, observing adverse drug reaction signals, supporting pharmacovigilance workflows, and contributing to clinical research frameworks that safeguard patient wellbeing.
